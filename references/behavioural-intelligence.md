# Behavioural Intelligence Foundations

## Scope
This file translates broadly used behavioural concepts into practical coaching principles. It is not a clinical protocol and does not establish diagnosis or treatment.

## Self-efficacy and mastery
People can build belief in their ability through successful attempts, observing useful examples, receiving credible feedback and learning to interpret difficulty. Prefer specific evidence ("you made the request and handled the response") over empty praise ("you can do anything").

## Implementation intentions
Turn a broad intention into a clear cue-and-action plan:
- "When [situation occurs], I will [specific action]."
Example: "When I finish reviewing the proposal tomorrow morning, I will send the prepared introduction email."

Do not create deadlines without the user's agreement.

## Friction reduction
Make the desired action easier to start:
- prepare materials;
- reduce unnecessary steps;
- draft the first sentence;
- choose a suitable time;
- remove avoidable distractions;
- define a small starting action.

Do not let friction reduction become endless preparation.

## Cognitive reappraisal
Help users examine interpretations and alternative explanations. Ask what evidence supports a prediction, what evidence complicates it and what other outcomes are plausible. Do not insist on positive thinking or deny real risks.

## Practice and exposure
For manageable fears, repeated voluntary practice can help users learn that discomfort is tolerable and outcomes may differ from predictions. Keep steps consensual, appropriately graded and within safe limits. This is educational support, not therapy or a substitute for clinician-guided treatment.

## Feedback and reflection
Review what happened, not just whether the user "succeeded." An action can be valuable even when the outcome is rejection, provided it was a sound, respectful attempt and the user learns from it.

## Limits
Do not claim that these principles guarantee confidence, success, income, opportunity or psychological change. Do not present this skill as a validated intervention or clinical treatment.
